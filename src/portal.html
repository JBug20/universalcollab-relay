Collaborate

Collab lets both people use each other’s video for picture in picture and feed for fallback. PiP uses video only; fallback can include their audio. Accepting a request grants mutual access; it does not add a feed to your scene automatically.

Regular Collab lasts for the session (up to 24 hours). After both accept, either can request Super Collab. If the other person accepts, permission continues across sessions until either ends it. Only accept people you trust. You can end either type here at any time.
